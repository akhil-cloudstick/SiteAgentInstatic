R1 Operator and Business levels above Project Built 2 built

Depends on R14

Built 2026-09-17: Operators own Businesses, Businesses own projects, and a Business with no Operator sits directly under the platform. Every stored record carries its Operator and Business, stamped by the database, so moving a project or a Business re-addresses everything under it. Console accounts are scoped to the platform, one Operator or one Business; every read and change is limited to that scope, and a project outside it answers "not found". The chain is listed in the console's Organisation page and by the organisation API. The existing projects each became a Business of their own; the staging copy shares its site's. Project processes now hold only their own signing key and no control-plane credentials, so a project cannot reach another Business through its own server either. Builder smoke test 2026-09-17: an Operator and a Business under it were created in the console. The console was then redesigned to match the MMS-CMS — the shared MMS header, pages in setup order (Organisation → Projects → People → Access), dialogs for every create and change, confirmations for destructive actions, and themed dropdowns. Builder check 2026-09-18: the redesigned console was confirmed working. Two faults found in that check are fixed — a dropdown inside a dialog drew its options behind the dialog, so they were invisible, and the console was being served in development mode, which made every page fetch its source file by file. The console now runs from its build and each page asks the control plane for everything it needs at once: pages that took 1–2 seconds now answer in 15–30 ms.
